FAQs

Emergency Dentist Questions, Answered

Quick answers to the questions patients ask us most often.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe or lasting toothache, a knocked-out or cracked tooth, swelling in the face or gums, a dental abscess, bleeding that won't stop, or a lost filling or crown with pain are all reasons to call an emergency dentist right away.

Are you available as a 24 hour dentist?

Yes. Emergency Dental Service is set up for urgent and after hours needs. Call 407-220-1457 and we will guide you on the fastest way to be seen.

Can I get a same day dentist appointment?

We prioritize urgent cases, so most emergency patients are seen the same day. Call us as soon as the problem starts so we can fit you in.

What should I do before I reach the dentist?

Rinse gently with warm water, use a cold compress on the cheek to reduce swelling, and avoid placing aspirin directly on the gums. If a tooth is knocked out, hold it by the crown, keep it moist in milk, and come in immediately.

Will I need a root canal or extraction for a bad toothache?

It depends on the cause. After an exam, we explain your options, which may include an emergency root canal, a filling or a tooth extraction, so you can choose the right treatment.
